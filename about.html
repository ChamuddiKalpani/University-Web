About Us

We help students learn from anywhere, with good teachers and flexible classes.

Our story

Northfield University started with one idea: everyone should be able to study without moving to a new city. We began with a few courses and a small group of lecturers, and today students join our live classes from all over the country.

Every course has recorded lectures, weekly live sessions and a lecturer who answers your questions. You learn at your own pace and never study alone.
